1. PACKAGING DECISION: mod-psychobot (module) + minimal core edits
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 - The AI lives in modules/mod-psychobot/ (rename our modules/mod-skeleton).
 - BUT the unavoidable bot-session/login/tick edits CANNOT be a pure drop-in
   module (same reason mod-playerbots needs a core fork). So mod-psychobot =
   MODULE for the AI + a SMALL set of core edits in our src/ (Phase A).
   This is the honest, correct shape (module-first, core-edits-where-required).

   (TrinityCore BfA 8.3) branch and put the AI under the module.

     modules/mod-psychobot/

       conf/mod_psychobot.conf.dist   (= ported aiplayerbot.conf keys)
       sql/                (playerbots DB: characters/world)
       mod-psychobot.cmake (extra includes/deps if needed)
       README.md
 - Loader: AddmodpsychobotScripts() (our module loader pattern) registers the
   CommandScript (.psychobot ...) + WorldScript that ticks the bot mgr.

3. V1 SCOPE - DECIDED (agent's call, per user)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 V1 = "ALTS AS BOTS" (player-summoned), NOT world random-bot population.
 Rationale:
   - Smallest path to a WORKING, testable bot (one master adds their own alts).
   - Avoids the huge RandomPlayerbotMgr population + gear/level/distribute system
     until the core foundation is proven (that's V2).
   - Matches the vertical-slice philosophy: prove login+tick+follow+combat+one
     class rotation before scaling to thousands of world bots.
 V1 deliverable: .psychobot add <alt> -> your alt logs in as a bot, follows you,
   fights with a real 8.3 rotation; works for >=1 class (DK first).
 V2 (later): RandomPlayerbotMgr open-world population + your Cata-Patch scaling
   features (SmartScale/BotActiveAlone/etc) + travel/quest at scale.
