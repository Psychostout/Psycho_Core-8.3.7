# RESEARCH & PLANNING: OPTION 1 - WORLD INTERACTION LAYER
# Goal: Move bots from "Combat Drones" to "Living Residents of Azeroth"

## 1. LOOT & INTERACTION
### A. Looting Logic
- Trigger: "LootableNearby" (Scans for nearby corpses/objects with loot).
- Action: `LootAllAction`. 
- Logic:
    1. When combat ends, check for `LootableNearby`.
    2. Move to the closest lootable object.
    3. Execute `LootAll` (simulated client packet/server-side loot roll).
    4. Repeat until no lootables remain.

### B. Vendor & Inventory Management
- Trigger: "InventoryFull" or "InCity".
- Action: `VendorSellAction`.
- Logic:
    1. Identify the nearest NPC with `NPC_FLAG_VENDOR`.
    2. Path to vendor.
    3. Iterate through inventory; identify items with `quality == ITEM_QUALITY_POOR` (Greys).
    4. Execute `SellItem` for all greys.
    5. (Optional) Purchase basic consumables (food/water) if gold permits.

### C. Questing Basics
- Logic: "Mirroring" the Master.
- Integration:
    1. Hook into `PlayerScript::OnQuestAccept` and `OnQuestTurnIn`.
    2. When the Master accepts a quest, the bot checks if it can accept it and does so.
    3. When the Master turns in a quest, the bot does the same.
    4. Non-mirroring logic: If the bot is "RPG mode", it can autonomously accept quests from NPCs it interacts with.

## 2. SURVIVAL INSTINCTS (FLEE/RETREAT)
### A. The "Panic" Trigger
- Trigger: "CriticalHealth" (HP < 20% or specific threshold).
- Logic:
    1. Break current combat rotation.
    2. Cast "Panic" spells (e.g., Ice Block, invisibility, or haste).
    3. Calculate retreat vector: `(MasterPosition - BotPosition).normalized() * distance`.
    4. Execute `MoveTo` retreat vector.
    5. If Master is too far, retreat to the nearest "SafePoint" (Inn or Guard).

### 3. IMPLEMENTATION PATH (S-EXTENSIONS)
- S-Ext1: `WorldInteractionActions` (Loot, Vendor, Quest).
- S-Ext2: `WorldInteractionTriggers` (LootNearby, InventoryFull, CriticalHealth).
- S-Ext3: `GenericWorldStrategy` (The glue that connects these to the Engine).
- S-Ext4: Wiring into `PsychobotAI` and `PsychobotMgr`.
