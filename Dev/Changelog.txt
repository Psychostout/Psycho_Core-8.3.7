 P-01 [BUILD] dep/cotire/CMake/cotire.cmake: cmake_minimum_required(2.8.12)
        CMake 4.x rejects minimums < 3.5. Must bump (e.g. to 3.5) or replace
        cotire. BLOCKS configure under CMake 4.3.2.

 P-02 [BUILD] cmake/macros/FindOpenSSL.cmake: OPENSSL_MAX_VERSION "1.2" cap
        rejects OpenSSL 3.x. Must raise cap (e.g. to "3.5") and adjust
        OPENSSL_EXPECTED_VERSION to "1.1.1". Required for OpenSSL 3 bump.

 P-03 [CORE] src/common/Cryptography/OpenSSLCrypto.cpp
        Legacy threading callbacks removed in OpenSSL 1.1+. Guard with
        #if OPENSSL_VERSION_NUMBER < 0x10100000L (make threadsSetup/Cleanup
        no-ops on 1.1+/3.x). Required for OpenSSL 3 bump.

 P-04 [CORE] src/common/Cryptography/RSA.cpp (~line 101)
        Direct opaque-struct access (_rsa->n) illegal on 1.1+/3.x.
        Replace with RSA_get0_key()/BN_copy of returned BIGNUM.
        Required for OpenSSL 3 bump.

 (P-02..P-04 to be executed together when we proceed with OpenSSL 3.x.)
